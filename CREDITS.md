## Not in this repository

`open`, `tents`, `clock`, `last` and `flares` are concept placeholders made from photographs of other organisers' events. They show identifiable people, and this project doesn't hold rights to them, so they stay on the organiser's computer and are excluded by `.gitignore`.

Without them, those sections show the dark background with their text. They're to be replaced by photographs from the test night in `SHOOT-PLAN.md`.
