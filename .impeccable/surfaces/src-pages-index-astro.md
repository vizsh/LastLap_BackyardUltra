Scope: the single-page concept site, visitor mode Persuade. Audience: Mumbai and Indian ultra runners arriving from social links, mostly on phones. Job: feel the format, believe the event is top tier, put a name on the start list. Constraints: hype stage, no invented date, fee, route or proof; four facts and one email field. Memorable moment: the flat ring tilts and becomes real streets, then 24 rings stack as tall as the towers.

Unresolved: event name (EK AUR is a working name), real route, sound, whether the public site shows real streets.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole site is one photograph exposed for 24 hours, and scrolling is time passing. It refuses the race-site arrangement of dark hero, countdown, stats row, schedule and FAQ.

OWN-WORLD: Warm ink black at page scale, oxblood smoke fields, Flare red reserved for what is still running, headlamp white as the rare hot core, sodium amber for shelter and dawn. Anek with a live width axis, Doto dot-matrix for numbers. Every glow has a source. No cards, no chrome: a thin 00 to 24 hour rail is the only navigation.

STORY: Visitors see one light start, understand the loop through a 3D route over the real township, watch the field die across a night, see one light left, then put their name down.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Near-total black. Huge "एक और" / "One more." as the single large element, a red LED countdown to 2 January 2027, marked tentative, thin hour rail on the left edge, one quiet "Put your name down" action. Before it, an opening choice: Event intro or Register now. No navigation bar.

FORM: Long-exposure light photography, candidate 7 on the ordered list, seed key f827d53d.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
