# LastLap test night: shoot plan

**Goal:** replace the five borrowed event photos and make the site genuinely yours in one evening, using phones, volunteers and private ground.

**Recommended date:** Saturday 21 November 2026, with a short sunrise pickup on Sunday 22 November.

**Why this date:** Diwali is on 8 November, so this avoids the festival crowds, firecracker smoke and haze. It also leaves two weeks for permissions and volunteers.

## The rules that shaped this plan

This is research, not legal advice. Confirm the current position locally, because some of these orders change month to month.

| Topic | What I found | What the plan does |
|---|---|---|
| **Flares and fireworks** | A [report](https://curlytales.com/mumbai-bans-firecrackers-high-intensity-lasers-on-public-roads-till-dec-what-you-need-to-know/) says a police order bans firecrackers and fireworks on public roads until 4 December 2026, plus high-intensity lasers and searchlights | **No flares at all.** Red LED lights and a small fog machine create the look on private ground |
| **Drones** | Mumbai has had repeated police bans, and flying needs [written police permission](https://www.indiatvnews.com/maharashtra/flying-of-drones-paragliders-hot-air-balloons-banned-in-mumbai-for-one-month-check-dates-and-reason-behind-it-2025-04-03-983714). One crew was [booked](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/mumbai/fort-shoot-drone-row-police-book-dhurandhar-2-location-manager-for-flying-drone-without-permission-in-high-security-zone) for flying without it | **No drone.** A tall rooftop gives the aerial shot |
| **Railway premises** | [Filming needs permission](https://lineproducersindia.in/?p=10289), and indicative fees run to about ₹1 lakh a day at top stations | **Not shooting there.** The stock train clip stays |
| **BMC parks and public roads** | Shoots go through the [ward office](https://lineproducersindia.in/?p=12099), with police as a separate track. Check whether your plans count as a commercial shoot | **Stay on private ground,** with the owner's written consent |

## Before the night

| By | Task |
|---|---|
| Mon 9 Nov | Choose the two locations below |
| Wed 11 Nov | Ask for written permission from the society or ground owner, and the rooftop |
| Sat 14 Nov | Confirm 10 to 15 volunteers and 2 camera people |
| Sat 14 Nov | Walk both locations at 19:00, and take test shots |
| Tue 17 Nov | Print bibs and consent forms, and test the lights and fog machine |
| Fri 20 Nov | Check the weather, and tell the watchman and neighbours |

**Two locations:**

- **Ground:** a private society compound, school ground or sports ground with a clear, safe stretch and towers behind it. Ask at Thakur Stadium or a large society.
- **Rooftop:** the highest terrace you can get legally and safely, ideally looking over a lit road and towers. Do not go near an unguarded edge.

**Team:** 2 camera people, 1 coordinator who only looks after volunteers, 1 person on lights, and 10 to 15 runners in dark kit.

**Kit:**

- 2 phones with free storage, power banks and a phone tripod each
- Headlamps for everyone, with spare batteries
- 2 or 3 battery LED panels that can go red, or strong red bike lights
- 1 small fog machine, only if the site owner agrees
- Garden sprayer for mist
- A big red clock: the LastLap race clock at `/clock`, on a laptop connected to a TV, or on a tablet
- String of warm bulbs, a pop-up tent, a folding table and a few chairs
- Plain printed LastLap bibs. No brand logos or other race clothing
- Water, snacks, first aid kit, torches, gaffer tape and consent forms

## Phone settings

**Photos**
- Use the main lens, never digital zoom. Wipe the lens first
- Shoot RAW if your phone offers it, such as ProRAW or Pro mode
- Use the tripod. Keep ISO as low as possible, and tap to lock focus and exposure
- For long exposures, use an app or Pro mode with a shutter of 4 to 15 seconds

**Video**
- 4K at 24 or 25 frames per second
- Lock exposure and focus. Don't let the phone brighten the scene by itself
- Hold each shot for at least 15 seconds, and move slowly

**Framing for the website**
- Shoot landscape first, then repeat the five Must shots in portrait for phones
- Leave the **bottom third quiet**, as the words sit there
- Leave the **top left** clear for the logo
- Keep it dark. Don't brighten the scene to see faces, because the shadows make the look
- Headlamps may point straight at the lens. That flare is the look

## People and consent

- Get a signed form from everyone recognisable. Over-18s only
- Nobody wears other race or brand clothing
- Headlamps should never be aimed into homes
- No running on public roads for this shoot

**Short consent wording, to review before use:**

> I agree that LastLap may photograph and film me on [date] at [place], and use those images on its website, social media and promotional material. I understand this was a staged test night and not a race. I may ask for my image to be removed from future use. Name, signature, date, phone.

**Keep the site honest:** these are staged photos of a race that hasn't happened yet. I'll add a small line such as "Photographed at a LastLap test night", and the site won't claim they show a past race.
